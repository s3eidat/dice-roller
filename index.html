Notation help

NdS rolls N dice with S sides (d20 = 1 die). Add or subtract dice and numbers: 2d6+1d4-1. Drop or keep: 4d6dl1 (or 4d6 drop lowest), dh drop highest, kh/kl keep highest/lowest. d% is d100. Limits: up to 100 dice per term, 2 to 1000 sides.
